Mini-Project 3: OCR Expense Tracker & Receipt Parser (Flutter & Dart)
Learning Objectives:
Develop a personal finance management app in Flutter with on-device AI.
Integrate Google ML Kit Text Recognition for offline receipt parsing.
Build regex heuristic parser to extract monetary totals, merchant names, and transaction dates.
Render interactive animated pie and bar charts using CustomPainter.
Prerequisites:
Flutter 3.x, Dart 3, Google ML Kit, SQLite / Isar, CustomPainter

Project Overview & Problem Scenario
Students and club treasurers frequently handle physical supermarket receipts and bills. Typing expense numbers into spreadsheets is tedious and error-prone.

📋 Core Functional Specifications:
Camera Capture & Image Cropping:
Live camera viewfinder with flash toggle, focus tap, and framing crop overlay.
On-Device Text Recognition & Regex Heuristics:
Integrates google_mlkit_text_recognition for offline, sub-100ms on-device text extraction (zero cloud cost).
Custom heuristic regex engine parsing total amounts (e.g. 150,000 VND, 150.000 đ), dates (DD/MM/YYYY), and merchant names.
Interactive review screen allowing manual edits before saving.
Local Database & Transaction Lifecycle:
Persistent storage using sqflite or isar with category classification (Food, Study, Travel, Gear, Entertainment).
Receipt thumbnail caching in application storage directory.
Custom Canvas Visualizations with CustomPainter:
Animated category distribution donut/pie chart and weekly spending bar chart drawn directly on canvas without third-party chart libraries.
